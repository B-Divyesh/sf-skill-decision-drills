Your drills stay yours.

Short version: No account, ads, or tracking. Your authored content and practice attempts stay in your browser unless you export them.

Data stored on your device

The app stores drill text, uploaded scenario images, choice feedback, hints, notes, and completed attempt selections in browser storage.

Data sent over the network

The app sends no drill or attempt content to us. The sample demo is also stored separately in your browser.

Images and exports

Scenario images are resized in your browser and stored locally. JSON and CSV exports are created on your device.

Retention and deletion

Data remains until you delete drills, clear this site's browser storage, or import a replacement backup. Export a backup before clearing storage.

Your choices

You can use the app without an account or personal information. Browser settings can remove all local data. For privacy questions, contact privacy@sociobot.in.

Practice is not proof. Decision drills support rehearsal and do not replace qualified instruction.
