Your drills stay yours.

Short version: Skill Decision Drills has no account, analytics, ads, or cloud sync. Your authored content and learner attempts stay in your browser unless you export them.

Data stored on your device

The app stores drill text, uploaded scenario images, choice consequences, hints, debriefs, and completed attempt selections in IndexedDB. If you purchase full authoring, your license token and a daily verification verdict are stored in localStorage.

Data sent over the network

The app sends no drill or attempt content to us. When you buy, checkout is hosted by Sociobot / Dodo, the merchant of record, under their checkout privacy terms. When a license is present, the token is sent to the Sociobot verification endpoint at most once per day. The service returns only license validity and does not receive your drills.

Images and exports

Scenario images are resized in your browser and stored locally. JSON and CSV exports are created on your device. You decide where those downloaded files go and who receives them.

Retention and deletion

Data remains until you delete drills, clear this site's browser storage, or import a replacement backup. Removing browser storage may be permanent, so export a backup first.

Your choices

You can use the complete free experience without a license or any personal information. Browser settings can remove all local data. For privacy questions, contact privacy@sociobot.in.
